About Cavalry Dental Care

Decades of surgical mastery, state-of-the-art clinical innovation, and an unwavering commitment to painless patient care in Lahore.

Our Philosophy

Combining Surgical Precision With Human Compassion

Founded by Senior Consultant Lt. Col. Dr. Ehsan Ul Haq, Cavalry Dental Care was created to raise the benchmark of dentistry in Pakistan. Situated in the heart of Cavalry Ground, Cantt Lahore, our facility offers an inviting environment where patients feel truly understood and valued.

We understand that visiting the dentist can bring anxiety. That is why our clinical practice revolves around minimally invasive techniques, transparent treatment planning, and 100% pain-free protocols for every age group.

Our Journey

Milestones of Excellence

Over three decades of continuous growth and clinical dedication in Lahore.

1989 — Clinical Foundation

Military Medical Service & Early Practice

Lt. Col. Dr. Ehsan Ul Haq began his distinguished medical dental career, establishing foundational protocols in oral surgery and trauma care.

2005 — Specialized Expansion

Advanced Implantology & Prosthetics

Introduced specialized titanium implant therapy and computer-milled crown restorations to private clinical practice.

2018 — Modern Facility Launch

Cavalry Ground Cantt Center

Established our flaghip center at Cantt Board Plaza No. 2 with hospital-grade autoclave equipment and digital diagnostics.

2026 — Comprehensive Care

Aesthetics & Multi-Specialty Dental Hub

Welcomed Dr. Taabir Ehsan and expanded into cosmetic smile makeovers, laser whitening, and digital clear aligner therapy.
